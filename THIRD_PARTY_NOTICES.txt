System Resources and External Tools
-----------------------------------

SuiSuiView may load an installed system font for CJK text fallback. Those
system fonts are read from the user's operating system and are not
redistributed by SuiSuiView.

Real-ESRGAN ncnn-vulkan support is optional. SuiSuiView lets the user choose
their own executable and model paths. SuiSuiView does not bundle Python,
PyTorch, Real-ESRGAN binaries, ncnn binaries, or model files.

NVIDIA Image Scaling
License: MIT
Copyright: Copyright (c) 2021, NVIDIA CORPORATION. All rights reserved.
Source: https://github.com/NVIDIAGameWorks/NVIDIAImageScaling

user-facing display mode yet.

Anime4K
License: MIT
Copyright: Copyright (c) 2019-2021 bloc97
Source: https://github.com/bloc97/Anime4K
  Note: The real-time display path includes a WGSL port derived from

  includes `Anime4K_Upscale_CNN_x2_M.glsl` for evaluation. The M variant
  remains benchmark-only until its larger pass graph is connected to the app
  paint path.

ACNetGLSL
License: MIT
Copyright: Copyright (c) 2020 TianZer
Source: https://github.com/TianZerL/ACNetGLSL

  The neural path predicts luma and the port merges source chroma in the final
  pass.
